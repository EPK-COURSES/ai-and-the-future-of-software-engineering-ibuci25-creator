## Abstract

With software development increasingly reliant on innovative technologies, there is a growing interest in exploring the potential of generative AI tools to streamline processes and enhance productivity. In recent years, companies like Microsoft, Google, and Meta have decided to incorporate artificial intelligence tools into their engineering workflows, affecting everything from the way we code to other things. This paper explores the goals of artificial intelligence, starting with how real-life companies have adapted their software engineering processes to it and the benefits and challenges they have faced. Through reviews of these various issues in this industry and reports published by companies, this research shows that although artificial intelligence significantly increases productivity and reduces time for simple, repetitive tasks, it also poses risks to code quality and changing developer roles. Overall, our research shows that artificial intelligence is not replacing software engineers but is changing what they do and what skills they need to have, so our research also opens up room for further exploration of software development and the integration of generative AI tools.

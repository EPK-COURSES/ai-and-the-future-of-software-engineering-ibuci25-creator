## Abstract

With software development increasingly reliant on innovative technologies, there is a growing interest in exploring the potential of generative AI tools to streamline processes and enhance productivity. (Coutinho et al., 2024\) In recent years, companies like Microsoft, Google, and Meta have decided to incorporate artificial intelligence tools into their engineering workflows, affecting everything from the way we code to other things. This paper explores the goals of artificial intelligence, starting with how real-life companies have adapted their software engineering processes to it and the benefits and challenges they have faced. Through reviews of these various issues in this industry and reports published by companies, this research shows that although artificial intelligence significantly increases productivity and reduces time for simple, repetitive tasks, it also poses risks to code quality and changing developer roles. Overall, our research shows that artificial intelligence is not replacing software engineers but is changing what they do and what skills they need to have, so our research also opens up room for further exploration of software development and the integration of generative AI tools.

## Introduction

## Software engineering has always changed throughout the tools that were available for developers, from the introduction of version control systems to the birth of cloud computing. Each technological change has required engineers to adapt by learning new skills, but today artificial intelligence represents  the largest part of the transformations that have been happening over the last decade. 

## AI in software development has moved beyond theory and now delivers real business value. Yet surprisingly, about two-thirds of organizations haven't started scaling AI across their operations. Change is happening faster than ever. Microsoft's 2025 Work Trend Index reveals that 81% of global leaders anticipate AI agents will blend into their organizations within the next 12-18 months. The numbers tell a compelling story—teams can cut their code generation, refactoring, and documentation time in half. Even more impressive, "frontier firms" have seen 30% more developer efficiency, 40% lower costs, and products reaching markets 50% faster. (Hurkova, 2025\) 

The emergence of artificial intelligence (AI) presents a new frontier in addressing some of the long-standing challenges in software engineering. AI’s relevance to software development is particularly significant due to its ability to learn patterns, make predictions, and automate complex tasks. Technologies such as machine learning, natural language processing, and neural networks are becoming instrumental in enhancing software engineering processes. AI-powered tools are enabling smarter automation, adaptive problem-solving, and decision-making capabilities, offering a promising avenue to optimize both the efficiency and effectiveness of software development. The integration of AI is not only transforming traditional workflows but also redefining the skill sets required for modern software engineering professionals. (Alenezi & Akour, 2025\) 

Despite the noticeable increase in adaptation, many questions remain.

* ## How are companies integrating artificial intelligence into their workflow?  

* ## What benefits have they seen? What challenges have they faced?  

* ## And what does this mean for software engineers working within these companies?

## By addressing these objectives, this study aims to provide a guide on how AI has been successfully integrated into software engineering practices in some of the world's largest technology companies. Not only by focusing on the theoretical part of what it can do, but the goal is also to understand what is actually happening within real teams in real companies by analyzing case studies, and the aim of this research is to offer an insight into how artificial intelligence is changing software engineering practices.

## Literature Review 

Recently, the relationship between artificial intelligence and software development has gained significant attention, both in scientific and academic research as well as in industry analysis in recent years. Some studies have attempted to measure the impact of artificial intelligence tools on a developer's productivity, workflows, and the quality of the generated code, with results that are promising and cautionary. One of the latest discoveries cited comes from research on GitHub Copilot, Microsoft's AI coding assistant, where studies published from 2023 to 2025 continuously found that developers who used Copilot completed their tasks faster than those who did not use such a tool, with several experiments showing productivity from 20 to 55% depending on the type of task. However, the same research said that AI-generated code requires review and understanding at the system level. Despite the enthusiasm, many researchers have raised concerns, among them Liang et al. (2024), who found that reliance on AI-generated suggestions over time can reduce the problem-solving skills of a developer, especially for junior engineers who accept the suggestion without fully understanding it.  
This literary summary shows the current advances and challenges in the integration of artificial intelligence, which offers real benefits but also presents responsibilities and challenges for engineers. This research is also built upon these findings by exploring how companies specifically manage these benefits and challenges.
